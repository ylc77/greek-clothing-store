# Client Legal Checklist

This checklist is for each new client before launch. It supports the basic legal pages template and does not replace legal, accounting, or tax review. A qualified legal, accounting, or tax advisor should review the final pages.

## Business Identity

- [ ] businessName: public trading name shown on the website
- [ ] legalName: registered legal entity or sole trader name
- [ ] businessAddress: full registered or operating address
- [ ] vatNumber: VAT / tax number, if applicable
- [ ] gemiNumber: GEMI number, if applicable
- [ ] country: country of operation
- [ ] contactEmail: customer support and privacy contact email
- [ ] phone: customer support phone number

## Data Controller

- [ ] dataControllerName: legal data controller name
- [ ] dataControllerAddress: legal data controller address
- [ ] Confirm who handles privacy requests
- [ ] Confirm the retention period for customer, order, POS, invoice, and support records

## Service Providers

Only list services that are actually enabled.

- [ ] dataProcessors: hosting, database, email, storage, monitoring, and operational providers
- [ ] paymentProviders: Stripe, Viva, myPOS, bank POS, or other enabled payment services
- [ ] analyticsProviders: PostHog, Google Analytics, Vercel Analytics, or other enabled analytics services
- [ ] aiProviders: OpenAI, DeepSeek, or other enabled AI providers
- [ ] Confirm that non-essential analytics or marketing cookies do not load before consent

## Clothing Store Sections

- [ ] Shipping Policy: delivery areas, courier, pickup, delivery times, shipping fees
- [ ] Return Policy: item condition, labels, packaging, return address, inspection process
- [ ] Refund Policy: refund method, timing, payment provider handling, rejected refund conditions
- [ ] 14-day withdrawal right: confirm how EU consumer withdrawal rules apply
- [ ] Confirm whether sales are direct website sales, Skroutz sales, in-store POS sales, or mixed

## Restaurant Sections

- [ ] Order Terms: ordering channel, confirmation, preparation time
- [ ] Cancellation Policy: when a food order can or cannot be cancelled
- [ ] Payment Terms: cash, card, online payment, failed payment handling
- [ ] Allergy / food availability disclaimer: allergens, substitutions, unavailable items
- [ ] Confirm delivery / pickup terms

## Cookies And Tracking

- [ ] Essential cookies identified
- [ ] Analytics cookies identified, if enabled
- [ ] Marketing cookies identified, if enabled
- [ ] Cookie Banner shows Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Manage preferences
- [ ] Non-essential cookies are blocked before consent

## Footer And Forms

- [ ] Footer links are visible: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy, Contact, Refund or Cancellation Policy
- [ ] Public customer-facing forms include Terms of Service and Privacy Policy notice
- [ ] Internal admin forms do not need public customer legal notices

## Final Review

- [ ] Client reviewed all legal pages
- [ ] Accountant reviewed tax, invoice, VAT, POS, and receipt wording
- [ ] Legal advisor reviewed consumer rights, privacy, returns, and cancellation wording
- [ ] lastUpdated is correct
- [ ] Pages are tested in the client languages required for launch
